İş görüşmesi, sunum veya müzakere türünü seç. Başlık, muhatap, beklenen sonuç, açılış, somut kanıt ve kapanış isteğini yaz. Her turu ayrı kaydet; süreni, öz değerlendirmeyi, notunu ve sonraki odağını aynı planın altında tut. Mesajı veya soruyu değiştirince eski kayıtların önceki taslağa ait olduğu görünür. Bu bir insan veya yapay zekâ muhatapla canlı görüşme değildir.

What belongs in an event plan?

Choose a job interview, presentation or negotiation. Write the title, audience, intended outcome, opening, specific evidence and closing ask. Choose an appropriate prepared question from 18 options for a supportive, skeptical or time-constrained counterpart. Your total time budget is divided between opening, response and closing.

Record each turn separately; keep duration, self-rating, reflection and a next focus under the same plan. When the message or question changes, older recordings are marked as belonging to an earlier draft. This is not a live conversation with a person or an AI counterpart.

Hesap oluşturmak zorunda değilsin. Geliştiricinin işlettiği bir kullanıcı verisi sunucusu veya ses arşivi yoktur. Prova kendi analitik veya davranış takibi hizmetini kullanmaz. 1. Developer and scope

Prova is a personal preparation and rehearsal app for professional conversations, developed by Kaan Arda Yaşar. Send support and privacy requests to ardakaan0066@gmail.com. This policy covers the app and this support page. Available features may vary by version and store configuration.

2. App data stored on your device

The app stores your interface and rehearsal language preferences, daily goal, favorites, program progress, event plans, custom scenarios, practice dates and durations, self-ratings and notes on your device. Plans contain the event title and type, audience, intended outcome, opening message, specific evidence, closing ask, selected counterpart style and prepared challenge, and time budget. History also stores information identifying the associated plan, turn and plan draft. Prepared questions and programs are included in the app; core preparation and rehearsal work offline.

No account is required. There is no developer-operated user data server or audio archive. Prova does not use its own analytics or behavioral tracking service. Plan text, recordings and practice notes are not sent to advertising services. Recordings are not automatically transcribed, translated or assessed using AI.

3. Microphone, recordings, and sharing

Microphone permission is requested only when you start recording audio. Practice without audio does not use the microphone. Audio files stay in the app’s storage on your device; recordings are not sent to the developer, Apple’s purchase service, or Google’s advertising service.

You can choose to share an individual saved recording using Share. You choose the recipient or sharing service. That recipient/service’s rules apply to the file afterward. In the web preview, sharing may work as a file download.

Your operating system’s device backup and cloud settings may also apply. Uninstalling the app, clearing device data, or losing your device can erase local recordings. Prova does not provide its own cloud synchronization service.

4. Local reminders

Daily reminders are optional. Enabling them requests system notification permission and schedules a notification on your device. You do not join a marketing list. Turn them off in the app or operating system settings. The web preview does not support local mobile notifications.

5. Metadata backup, import and deletion

A JSON metadata backup contains preferences, event plans, custom scenarios, practice durations, ratings and notes. It does not contain audio files. You control where an exported file is stored or shared.

Importing a backup replaces existing app data and deletes existing recordings on that device. A metadata backup cannot restore those recordings. Reminders remain off after import until you enable them again.

Deleting a plan preserves its rehearsal history and recordings; delete individual practices separately in history. Delete all local data in Settings to clear plans, history, recordings, notes, favorites, custom scenarios and program progress. Copies shared or downloaded elsewhere need to be deleted separately. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

6. Apple purchases

In an enabled store build, Apple manages purchases and subscriptions. Prova does not receive payment card details. The app uses Apple purchase/subscription status to check eligible Pro access and restore purchases. Apple’s terms and privacy practices apply to store transactions. Real subscriptions cannot be purchased in web or Expo Go previews.

7. Conditional advertising and your choices

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s consent management (UMP) are used only in mobile builds with advertising enabled. These services may process approximate location inferred from an IP address, device identifiers, app/ad interactions, crash and performance diagnostics, and consent status. Google may use this information for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, and security. The advertising service does not receive Prova plan text, recordings or personal practice notes.

Prova requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is processed or used for tracking. In the iOS version being prepared for release, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission may be requested only in an eligible advertising flow after a completed practice. If you allow it, Google may use your device identifier to measure advertising across other apps and websites. Google’s advertising SDK is not initialized and ads are not requested without ATT authorization. Denied, restricted, or failed permission checks keep ads disabled; free practice and access to your own data remain available. UMP consent is checked separately and does not replace ATT authorization. Manage choices through Ad privacy options in Prova Settings and iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Permission is checked again when the app returns to the foreground. Privacy and reporting links also accompany ads.

Ads may appear only on the results screen after a practice is completed; they are not shown during recording or in practice history. Eligible Pro access provides an ad-free experience. Live ads are not shown in builds with ads disabled, web/Expo Go previews, or when necessary permission cannot be obtained. Google may process data internationally under its own policy. Google’s privacy policy · Google SDK data disclosure.

View, export a metadata backup of, and delete local data in the app. Send requests for access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence received by the developer to the support email. Applicable data protection and consumer rights remain unaffected.
