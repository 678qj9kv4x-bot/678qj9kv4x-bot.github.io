Luvoka beta privacy

What data is used
while you test?

Kaan Arda YASAR is the controller. On-device learning, Apple TestFlight, support email and test-service data are explained below.

Effective for this controlled test scope: 1 November 2026.

1. Learning records on your device

Kaan Arda YASAR operates Luvoka personally. There is no app account or app-provided cloud sync. Language, the entered learning profile/goal, completed core lessons, review cards and the latest 100 core sessions stay on the device.

Program records contain units, question-accuracy counters, first baseline/final-check scores and the latest 100 program sessions. Unit completions are kept separately from that limit. Bonus balance and the latest 100 app-generated reward identifiers are also local, not payment receipts. They show progress, resume learning and select reviews from prepared questions.

Exercise answers are checked on the device against prepared phrases; program records save question/results rather than raw typed text. There is no live cloud AI, microphone recording or pronunciation scoring. Spoken examples use an available English voice on the device.

2. When you use Apple TestFlight

Apple automatically collects beta usage/crash data and shares it with the developer; this cannot be opted out of. It includes invitation, version, device/OS, session and error information. Email invitations may show name/email; public-link-only invitations do not. Submitted feedback/attachments are also shared. The developer uses these to improve the beta and does not forward them to third parties. Apple TestFlight & Privacy.

This Apple flow is separate from local learning records. Remove personal information from screenshots shared through TestFlight or email.

3. Purchase testing being prepared

Purchase testing is not yet complete. Once enabled, it will use only a verified Apple test environment. Properly configured TestFlight/Apple sandbox purchases do not incur real charges; do not try an unverified payment flow. Test access is not a real purchase carried into production. Apple test purchases.

When tests are enabled, Apple and RevenueCat may process test product/transaction and receipt information, a randomly generated anonymous app identity, access/history, device/OS and connection data such as IP. Purposes include purchase verification, restoration, product functionality and related analytics. Learning profiles, raw answers and age information are not sent as custom customer attributes. The app does not receive payment-card details or your Apple password. RevenueCat privacy information.

4. Advertising and age information

This controlled test version shows no ads. Google Mobile Ads/UMP components are bundled; disabled ads do not guarantee zero network/data processing by every component. Startup verification is ongoing. Google components may process connection/IP, device identifiers, interactions and diagnostics/performance information. Google SDK data disclosure.

If Apple's age-range system is used for a required test action, the app does not request a birth date, save a shared raw range or add age fields to Google/RevenueCat. Only a coarse eligibility result is held temporarily. Unsupported/refused outcomes stop the new action while free learning remains available. Apple may manage its own consent/cache records.

5. Support, retention and deletion

Support email processes your sender address, message and chosen attachments to answer the issue or privacy request. The mailbox uses consumer Gmail under Google's own terms/privacy policy. Google Privacy Policy.

Messages/attachments for resolved Luvoka requests become eligible for deletion 90 days after closure and are deleted during monthly review. Open requests and necessary minimal legal/request records are handled separately. If the developer downloads TestFlight feedback/crash copies into a support case, their necessity is reviewed with that case. This cleanup does not end all retention by Apple, Google or other providers; Apple's own TestFlight retention is described in its notice.

A successful confirmed reset clears core lesson, program and bonus records; an error means it is not complete. It does not delete email, Apple/RevenueCat/Google records or OS backups. Restoring purchases does not recover local learning history or bonus credits.

6. This site, contact and requests

These static pages contain no form, analytics script, advertising or site cookie/local-storage code. GitHub Pages logs/stores visitor IP addresses for security. Email links open your mail app without automatically sending. GitHub Pages data disclosure.

Send support or data questions/requests to ardakaan0066@gmail.com. Minimal verification may be needed to match the request to the right record; an email is not assumed to satisfy every formal legal-request requirement. Do not send raw receipts or account credentials.

Providers may process data in different countries. Provider roles, transfer mechanisms and the production release's legal scope are being reviewed separately. This test notice does not replace a general App Store production policy or certify compliance in every country.
