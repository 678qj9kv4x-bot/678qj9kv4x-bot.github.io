Frequently asked questions

Before you continue testing.

What does TestFlight share?

TestFlight has its own data-sharing rules. See the beta privacy notice for details.

Does purchase testing charge real money?

Verified TestFlight/Apple sandbox tests do not incur real charges. Testing is ongoing; one pack's result does not mean every product has passed. Report an unexpected payment screen to support. Test access is not a promise of real, permanent ownership in a future App Store release; see the privacy notice for scope.

I cannot hear audio.

Check the available English voice, volume and silent mode on your device. You can continue with written examples; tell us your device/iOS version.

My progress is missing on another device.

Learning records stay on this device; there is no app account or cloud sync. Resetting local data clears learning records, not Apple/RevenueCat records or operating-system backups.

How long are support emails kept?

Messages and attachments for resolved Luvoka requests become eligible for deletion 90 days after closure and are deleted at the monthly review. Open requests and necessary minimal legal/request records are handled separately. See the privacy notice for details.
